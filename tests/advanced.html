Manual feature checklist
  1. Pin and collapse an entry, reload the page, then verify both settings persist.
  2. Focus a field, search for a value, use ↑/↓ and Enter to fill it, then Undo.
  3. Choose Fill form, resolve the ambiguous email match, and apply. Verify the separate form stays unchanged.
  4. Fill the rich editor, then Undo and verify its original bold text returns.
  5. Export a backup, change a value, then import it. Review the preview before testing merge, replace, and keep-existing.
  6. Set a website on an entry and try another origin. Toggle Show entries for all websites.
  7. Open Settings and switch Auto / English / Русский. Check menus and another open tab.
  8. Change a field association, verify its explanation in suggestions, then clear it.
